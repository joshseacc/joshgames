Pokémon Phonics!

Let's learn the '-ed' and '-s' ending sounds!

Pikachu (Voiced)

Your vocal cords vibrate!

'-ed' ➔ sounds like /d/ (e.g. called)
'-s' ➔ sounds like /z/ (e.g. spills)

Meowth (Voiceless)

Your vocal cords are quiet!

'-ed' ➔ sounds like /t/ (e.g. looked)
'-s' ➔ sounds like /s/ (e.g. laughs)

Team Rocket (t & d)

The special '-ed' rule!

'-ed' ➔ sounds like /id/
(e.g. sounded, hunted)

Charizard (Hissing)

Words ending in s, z, ch, sh, x!

'-s/-es' ➔ sounds like /iz/
(e.g. catches, boxes)
